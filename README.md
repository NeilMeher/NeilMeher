# Neil Meher

I'm interested in economics, finance, and how we can test claims about AI behavior. My projects use public data, simple models, and reproducible experiments to connect a result to the evidence behind it.

## Featured projects

- [RBI Policy Statement Benchmark](https://github.com/NeilMeher/rbi-policy-benchmark) Can simple rules and a learned model recover the RBI's policy decision and its supporting sentence? Both methods found the right decision and evidence on 12 of 12 held-out digests. The held-out meetings included cuts and holds.
- [FOMC Market Reversals](https://github.com/NeilMeher/fomc-market-reversals) matches FOMC statements and press conferences to changes in the two-year Treasury yield. The press-conference move reversed the statement move in 19 of 49 eligible meetings.
- [M&A Valuation Reconstruction](https://github.com/NeilMeher/ma-valuation-reconstruction) audits the disclosures for eight cash deals. For TaskUs, the reconstructed Case A midpoint was $15.30 per share versus Evercore’s $15.28.
- [Barrier Option Monte Carlo](https://github.com/NeilMeher/barrier-option-monte-carlo) compares monitoring methods for a synthetic barrier option. With 16 dates and 32,768 paths, the Brownian-bridge correction reduced pricing RMSE from $2.462 to $0.078.
- [Slingshot News: Trace the Claim](https://github.com/NeilMeher/slingshotnews) What changes when you read an economic headline alongside the original release? One example claims that the RBI cut its repo rate as it switched to neutral. The October 2024 release shows that the stance changed and the repo rate stayed at 6.50%.
- [Can a Model Tell When It’s Wrong?](https://github.com/NeilMeher/model-confidence) Does a misleading hint change how often a learned model is right and how sure it seems? In 60 matched arithmetic pairs, accuracy dropped from 43 correct to zero while average confidence rose from 44% to 79%.

## Project index

### Monetary policy and inflation

- [India Inflation in Real Time](https://github.com/NeilMeher/india-inflation-realtime) reconstructs when Indian inflation releases were available and measures how data leakage distorts a forecast evaluation.
- [Why Do Forecasters Disagree?](https://github.com/NeilMeher/inflation-forecast-archive) compares central-bank and professional inflation forecasts for the same dates and quarters.
- [Monetary Policy Voting Explorer](https://github.com/NeilMeher/mpc-voting-explorer) traces 30 member votes and tests a simple classifier on held-out meetings.
- [Inflation for Different Households](https://github.com/NeilMeher/household-inflation) uses official category rates to compare the inflation experienced by fictional household budgets.

### Corporate finance and public filings

- [Merger Spread Lab](https://github.com/NeilMeher/merger-spread-lab) uses sourced dates and observed prices to audit three deal events.
- [LBO Return Attribution](https://github.com/NeilMeher/lbo-return-attribution) attributes returns in a TaskUs-based hypothetical buyout with synthetic financing.
- [Evidence-Linked Annual Report Explorer](https://github.com/NeilMeher/annual-report-explorer) searches selected Apple and Alphabet filing tables and returns the value, reporting period, and page.

### Financial markets and risk

- [Treasury Yield Forecast](https://github.com/NeilMeher/treasury-yield-forecast) compares dynamic Nelson–Siegel, autoregression, and no-change forecasts on held-out Treasury curves.
- [Treasury Fire-Sale Simulator](https://github.com/NeilMeher/treasury-fire-sale) models how leverage and dealer capacity affect cascades in a synthetic Treasury market.
- [USD/JPY Carry Lab](https://github.com/NeilMeher/usdjpy-carry-lab) replays the August 2024 unwind and examines leverage and margin in resampled returns.
- [Option Tails and SVI](https://github.com/NeilMeher/option-tails-svi) compares risk-neutral tail estimates on synthetic option quotes with known tail probabilities.
- [Mortgage Convexity](https://github.com/NeilMeher/mortgage-convexity) measures the rate and prepayment sensitivity of an illustrative mortgage pool.
- [Loan Portfolio Stress-Test Simulator](https://github.com/NeilMeher/loan-stress-test) tests how correlated defaults affect extreme losses in a synthetic credit portfolio.

### Public budgets, news, and language

- [Does a Budget Claim Add Up?](https://github.com/NeilMeher/budget-claim-check) checks 25 published Union Budget figures and shows the underlying cells and arithmetic.
- [Bilingual Economic News Benchmark](https://github.com/NeilMeher/bilingual-econ-benchmark) measures whether extraction preserves figures, units, dates, and direction across 25 English–Hindi release pairs.
- [Economic Headlines Reader Study Toolkit](https://github.com/NeilMeher/economic-headlines-reader-study) tests the effect of leading with the change versus the level in 12 matched headline pairs. The published responses are synthetic.
